# Rule conventions

Follows Mahjong Time's scoring tables ([part 1](https://mahjongtime.com/mahjong-taiwanese-scoring.html),
[part 2](https://mahjongtime.com/mahjong-taiwanese-scoring-2.html)). These conventions
resolve unspecified overlaps and edge cases:

- Only the highest concealed-triplet, dragon, wind, or early-win tier applies.
- Fully concealed replaces concealed-hand and self-draw bonuses. Exposed-hand
  replaces pair-wait; five exposed sets qualify on either a draw or discard.
- The combined no-honors/no-flowers bonus replaces both individual bonuses.
  The clean chow bonus replaces chow and all absence bonuses.
- Heavenly/earthly replace early-win bonuses. Other applicable patterns add,
  including honor pungs within dragon/wind patterns.
  “Limit hands” do not impose a cap or suppress unrelated patterns.
- Clear-dragon concealment depends on its three chows, not unrelated sets.
  A claimed winning tile exposes its completed set for pattern bonuses,
  but does not open the hand for concealed-hand scoring.
- A one-chance chow is a middle/edge wait in the selected arrangement, regardless
  of other arrangements or visible copies.
- Original-hand readiness requires no declared sets, including concealed kongs,
  and cannot accompany a heavenly win.
